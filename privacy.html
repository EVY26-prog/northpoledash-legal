North Pole Dash – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

North Pole Dash (the "Game") is made by the Developer ("we", "us" or "our"). The Game is designed for players of all ages, including children. We collect as little information as possible, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or knowingly collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, photos, contacts, precise location, or any other information that identifies you personally. The Game has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking tools, no chat, and no way to share content with other players.

2. Information the Game uses

Game progress. Your scores, settings, progress and any name you type in are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. If you delete the Game, this data is deleted too.

Leaderboards. When you submit a score, the Game sends our server a randomly generated player ID, your score, the number of levels you reached and the date. Other players see your score only under a fun name, such as "Elf 2", which is created on each player’s device when the leaderboard is shown and is not sent to our server. The name you type in is never sent to our server or shown to other players. The player ID is not linked to your real name, email, Apple or Google account, or any device identifier.

IP addresses. Like any internet service, our server receives your device's IP address when a score is submitted. We use it only to deliver scores and prevent abuse, and we delete it within 7 days.

No analytics or tracking. The Game does not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking tools, and does not access advertising identifiers.

3. How we use information

We use the information above only to run the Game, display leaderboards, prevent cheating and fix problems. We do not use it for advertising, marketing or profiling.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Game (such as our leaderboard hosting provider, Cloudflare), who may use it only to provide those services to us, or where the law requires it.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for children and is designed so that children can play without providing personal information. Any name a child types into the Game stays on their device and is never sent to us. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), as defined by laws such as the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU and UK GDPR.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, for example in an email to us, contact us at info@edvanceyou.com. We will delete it promptly.

6. Data retention

Leaderboards are updated daily. Leaderboard entries (player ID, score, levels reached and date) are deleted automatically after no more than 7 days. IP addresses are handled as described in section 2.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited information we hold. No system is completely secure, but because we do not collect personal details, very little information could be exposed.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete information about you. You can:

  • delete all on-device data, including your random player ID, by uninstalling the Game;

  • contact us at info@edvanceyou.com with any privacy request (because leaderboard entries contain no real names, we may not be able to identify yours, but all entries are deleted automatically within 7 days); and

  • contact your local data protection authority if you have concerns.

9. International users

We are based in Australia. Leaderboard information is processed by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, on servers that may be located in Australia or other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version within the Game and update the effective date above. If we make a significant change, such as starting to collect new types of information, we will provide notice in the Game or on its Apple App Store and Google Play listings before the change takes effect.

11. Contact us

Contact us at info@edvanceyou.com
