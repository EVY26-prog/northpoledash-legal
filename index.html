North Pole Dash – Support

Thanks for playing North Pole Dash! Pick your rider and your ride, then dash through a snowy Christmas village to deliver presents to the North Pole Workshop.

Contact us

For help, feedback or any question about the Game, email us at info@nthpoledash.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

When you write, it helps to tell us your device model (for example iPhone 15 or Pixel 8), its system version, and what happened.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a refund?
All payments are handled by Apple or Google, so we can't issue refunds directly. On iPhone or iPad, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use Google Play's refund options in your order history.

Do I need an account or internet connection?
No. The Game works offline and has no accounts, ads or in-app purchases.

Will I lose my progress?
Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the Game also deletes your progress.

How do I steer by tilting my device?
Turn on Tilt to steer in Settings. You can also re-centre or reverse it there.

Why are Advent Calendar doors locked?
A new door opens each day from 1 to 24 December, and doors stay open for catch-up until 6 January.
